About

The Arguments is a team built on curiosity, determination, and constant learning. We love solving problems, trying new ideas, and supporting each other through every challenge. Everything we build, test, and improve comes from teamwork and creativity.

Robot

Our robot is designed with precision in mind. We create custom attachments, test different builds, and refine our coding to make each mission as consistent as possible. Every run teaches us something new, and we improve through iteration and teamwork.

Gracious Professionalism

We believe in helping others, competing with respect, and always learning from every team we meet. Gracious Professionalism means staying kind, honest, and supportive — even when things get challenging. We lift others up while doing our best.

Core Values

Our team lives by the core values of discovery, innovation, impact, inclusion, teamwork, and fun. These guide everything we do — from building robots to solving problems to celebrating our progress.
